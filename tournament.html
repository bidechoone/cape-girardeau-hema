how we fence

Rules

These rules apply to both events. Gear is checked before your first bout, and you can't fence without it. All gear must also meet the HEMA Alliance Safety Policy.

Match Duration & Win Conditions

  • Pool Bouts: Conducted for 3 minutes of fighting time OR up to a maximum of 9 points.
  • Elimination Bouts: Conducted for 3 minutes of fighting time OR up to a maximum of 11 points.
  • Medal Matches: Conducted to 11 points and must be won by a margin of 2 clear points.

Scoring Target Values

  • 3 Points: Clean hits to the Head or Torso.
  • 2 Points: Clean hits to the Arms or scoring Pommel Strikes.
  • 1 Point: Clean hits to the Legs.
  • 4 Points: Executing a fully controlled Disarm.
  • 2 Points: Forcing an opponent into a Ring Out.

Afterblow & Double Hits Protocol

  • This tournament utilizes a highly punitive subtractive afterblow protocol.
  • If a fencer lands a clean initial strike and receives an afterblow, the afterblow's target value is subtracted from the initial fencer's score.
  • Double Hits (simultaneous or near-simultaneous bilateral strikes where no distinct priority or afterblow window can be determined) score 0 points for both fighters and penalize both fighters' overall standings tracking.

Division Weapon Specifications

  • Steel Longsword Open: Must be steel fencing longswords built specifically for historical fencing. Broken or retrofitted blades are strictly banned. All weapons must be properly tipped (rubber tips highly preferred).
  • Beginners Synthetic Longsword: Must be a standard nylon/synthetic tournament longsword (e.g., Purpleheart/Pentti or Blackfencer). Steel weapons are strictly prohibited in this division. Swords must have a safe, flexible tip and secure crossguard.

Mandatory Gear & Safety Requirements

  • Body Coverage: 100% full skin coverage is mandatory across all divisions; zero bare skin may be exposed.
  • Steel Division Gear: 350N puncture-resistant HEMA jacket, fencing mask with fitted back-of-head protection, rigid gorget, groin protection, heavy steel-longsword rated fencing gloves (e.g., Sparring Gloves, Hoof/clamshell style), hard elbow/knee protectors, and rigid shin guards.
  • Synthetic Division Gear: Follows the HEMA Alliance Safety Policy for tournament synthetic weapons. All tournament safety gear requirements apply, with additions of:
    • Hands: Sturdy gloves or gauntlets must be used to protect the hands and wrists. Gloves must include protection on the sides and tips of the fingers sufficient to resist hard strikes from steel or synthetic weapons while holding the weapon.
      • Pre-approved list of glove manufacturers for their heavy / longsword models: St. Mark’s, Sparring Gloves, Neyman Fencing, Destroyer Modz, SPES.
      • “Homebrew” or modified gloves are acceptable, as long as they meet the above requirements. Tournament organizers have full discretion to disallow any glove they deem as unsafe.
      • It is recommended that steel gauntlets have additional interior padding of some sort. A mechanic’s-style glove has minimal padding and when used inside a metal gauntlet, and thus would not be sufficient to meet this recommendation.
    • Forearms: Forearm protection is recommended, but not required.
