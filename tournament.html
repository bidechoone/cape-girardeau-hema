safety

Required gear

Both events follow the tournament gear requirements in the HEMA Alliance Safety Policy. Gear is checked before your first bout, and you can't fence without it.

For the Steel Open, your jacket must also be padded and puncture-resistant. A T-shirt or sweatshirt is not enough.

Head
Fencing mask with back-of-head protection
Throat
Rigid gorget covering the front of the neck
Hands
Heavy longsword gloves, such as SPES, Sparring Gloves or Neyman
Joints
Hard elbow and knee protection
Torso
Jacket with no gaps at the chest or armpits
Groin
Cup required where it applies; groin protection recommended for everyone
Legs and feet
Shin guards unless shins are off target, shoes, and no large areas of exposed skin
